**Phone Locker**

A simple Arduino-based phone locker that uses a servo motor to physically lock and unlock a phone compartment for a selected amount of time.

**Features**

Choose a 30, 45, or 60 minute lock duration.
Servo-controlled locking mechanism.
Red LED indicates that the locker is locked.
Green LED indicates that the locker is unlocked.
Blue LED indicates that the reset button is being held.
Buzzer sounds when the timer finishes.
Selected lock duration is saved to EEPROM, allowing the lock state to survive a power loss.
Hold the reset button for 5 seconds to force an unlock.

**Hardware**

Arduino-compatible board.
Servo motor.
5 buttons for:
30 minutes,
45 minutes,
60 minutes
Start,
Reset.
Red LED.
Green LED.
Blue LED.
Buzzer.
Suitable resistors and wiring.
